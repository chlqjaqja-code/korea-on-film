ABOUT THE TOOL

Posts on these accounts are prepared with a small desktop tool built for this account only. It reads the EXIF data from the photographer's own photographs, renders the caption card and the video, and publishes to the photographer's own accounts. It does not read, collect, or analyse anyone else's data, and it is not offered to or used by anyone else.

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload to the photographer's own YouTube channel. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.
